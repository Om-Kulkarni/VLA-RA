# Research Summary: Cosmos Policy

**Core Contribution**
*   **Problem:** Leveraging the spatiotemporal priors of large video foundation models for robot control has required complex, multi-stage adaptations and new architectural modules.
*   **Key Claim:** A **single-stage fine-tuning** approach can adapt a pretrained video model (Cosmos-Predict2) into a high-performance robot policy with integrated world modeling and value prediction, **without any architectural changes**.

**Technical Details**
*   **Architecture:** Fine-tunes the **Cosmos-Predict2-2B** latent video diffusion model (Diffusion Transformer). No new components are added.
*   **Key Mechanism: Latent Frame Injection.** Novel modalities (robot proprioception, action chunks, state values) and multi-view camera images are **encoded as latent frames** and inserted into the model's native diffusion sequence. This allows the model to learn `p(a, s', V(s') | s)` within its original denoising objective.
*   **Training:** Jointly trains policy, world model, and value function on robot demonstration data. 50% of batch for policy (`p(a, s', V(s') | s)`), 50% for world model/value (`p(s', V(s') | s, a)` and `p(V(s') | s, a, s')`).
*   **Planning:** Uses a **dual-model deployment**. A base "policy model" proposes actions. A separate "planning model," fine-tuned on policy rollout data, predicts future states and values for **best-of-N sampling** (ensembled for robustness).

**Experimental Results**
*   **Simulation Benchmarks (SotA):**
    *   **LIBERO:** 98.5% avg success (vs. 97.4% for CogVLA).
    *   **RoboCasa:** 67.1% avg success (vs. 66.4% for FLARE/GR00T-N1.5+ HAMLET). Achieved with only **50 demos/task** vs. 300+ for most baselines.
*   **Real-World Hardware:** Bimanual ALOHA robot (2x ViperX 300 arms, 3 cameras).
*   **Real-World Tasks:** Achieved highest average score (93.6%) on 4 challenging bimanual tasks (e.g., folding shirt, placing candy in ziploc bag), outperforming fine-tuned VLAs (π 0.5, OpenVLA-OFT+).
*   **Planning Gains:** Model-based planning (`V(s')`) improved average score by **12.5 points** on the two most challenging real-world tasks versus the base policy.
*   **Key Finding:** Video model priors provide a stronger foundation for handling **high action multimodality and precision** than VLA models fine-tuned on large robot datasets.

**Relevance to Our Research**
*   **VLA Models:** Presents a **video-diffusion alternative** to the dominant VLA (vision-language) backbone paradigm, leveraging **spatiotemporal priors** instead of static image-text semantics.
*   **Bimanual Manipulation:** Demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on real-world, long-horizon bimanual tasks.
*   **Foundation Models for Robotics:** Directly adapts a **large-scale, pretrained video foundation model** (Cosmos-Predict2) for robotic control, validating the transfer of Internet video priors.
*   **Imitation Learning:** A simple, **single-stage fine-tuning** method that outperforms complex multi-stage adaptations. Shows the value of **auxiliary supervision** (jointly predicting future state/value) for policy learning.
*   **Priority Labs:** Research from **NVIDIA** and **Stanford University**.

**Implementation Readiness**
*   **High.** Code, model checkpoints, and training data are released on the [project website](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dir/cosmos-policy/).
*   **Repo Provides:** Full training and evaluation scripts. Implementation details for latent frame injection and joint training are provided in the appendix.
*   **Considerations:** Model-based planning inference is slow (~5 sec/action chunk). Requires collecting policy rollout data for effective planning model fine-tuning.
