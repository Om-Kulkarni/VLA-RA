# Central Research Manifesto

## Overarching Goal
Identify, acquire, and analyze cutting-edge research in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, Foundation Models for Robotics, and Imitation Learning.

## Core Interests
- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models:** End-to-end architectures directly mapping multimodal inputs to robotic actions.
- **Bimanual Manipulation:** Coordinated dual-arm robotic tasks and corresponding control strategies.
- **Foundation Models for Robotics:** Generalist models trained on cross-embodiment datasets intended for zero-shot or few-shot transfer.
- **Imitation Learning & Behavioral Cloning:** Advanced techniques for efficiently learning from human demonstrations.
- **Code & Reproducibility:** High priority assigned to papers that release open-source codebases, model weights, or project sites.

## Evaluation Criteria
Research must align closely with these core domains. Papers purely focused on NLP, standard computer vision without action grounding, or classical control methods without learned representations are considered out of scope unless they present a paradigm shift applicable to VLA architectures.
